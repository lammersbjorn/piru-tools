# Privacy

These tools never see your data.

- Every page runs entirely in your browser. Your Piru backup is read on your device and never uploaded.
- The pages make no network requests. Each page sets a Content-Security-Policy that blocks all connections, external scripts, fonts and images.
- There are no analytics, cookies or trackers. The only thing stored is your language choice (English or Français), kept in your browser's local storage.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request data, such as your IP address, when you load a page; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Your backup is never part of that request.

Questions or problems: [open an issue](https://github.com/lammersbjorn/piru-tools/issues).
